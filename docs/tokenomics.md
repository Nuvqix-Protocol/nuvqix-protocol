# Tokenomics

Nuvqix Protocol has a proposed maximum supply of:

**100,000,000,000 NVQ**

## Allocation

| Allocation | Percentage | Tokens |
|---|---:|---:|
| Community Airdrop | 35% | 35,000,000,000 |
| Development | 20% | 20,000,000,000 |
| Liquidity & Market Reserve | 10% | 10,000,000,000 |
| Treasury | 13% | 13,000,000,000 |
| Ecosystem Rewards | 10% | 10,000,000,000 |
| Team & Advisors | 10% | 10,000,000,000 |
| Marketing | 2% | 2,000,000,000 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100,000,000,000** |

## Community Airdrop

35% of the maximum supply is proposed for community airdrops and community distribution.

Potential eligibility signals may include:

- Mini App activity
- Verified tasks
- Daily participation
- Referrals
- Community participation
- Future ecosystem activities

Final eligibility rules, anti-bot controls, snapshot timing and claim schedule will be published before the relevant distribution.

## Development

20% is proposed for development, including:

- Engineering
- Infrastructure
- Mini App development
- Wallet integrations
- Security
- Smart-contract work
- Analytics
- Servers
- Testing
- Future product modules

## Liquidity & Market Reserve

10% is proposed for:

- DEX liquidity
- CEX liquidity requirements
- Market infrastructure
- Liquidity management

The full reserve does not necessarily need to enter liquidity pools at launch.

## Treasury

13% is proposed for:

- Long-term operations
- Strategic partnerships
- Ecosystem grants
- Security
- Liquidity support
- Legal/compliance expenses
- Future development needs

## Ecosystem Rewards

10% is proposed for:

- Campaigns
- User incentives
- Community competitions
- Partnerships
- Contributor programs
- Future ecosystem modules

## Team & Advisors

10% is proposed for the team and advisors.

A vesting policy should be finalized and published before material team allocation becomes transferable.

## Marketing

2% is allocated to:

- Community campaigns
- Content
- PR
- Creator partnerships
- Exchange listings
- Promotional activities
- Educational materials
